Interpretive Activity

Build a Soldier's Load

December 1862. Soldiers marching toward Prairie Grove carried nearly everything they needed to survive in the field. Clothing, blankets, ammunition, food, shelter, and personal keepsakes all competed for limited space.

Every additional pound made a march more difficult. Soldiers constantly balanced comfort, necessity, and survival while deciding what to carry.

As a Civil War soldier, choose the equipment you think was worth carrying. Can you assemble a realistic marching load weighing exactly sixty pounds?

Historical Note: The equipment shown here is based on items commonly carried by Civil War soldiers. Major gear weights are adapted from historical load analyses, while several small-item weights have been simplified for educational gameplay.
